## 1. Executive Summary

CargoFlow is a full-lifecycle import/export ERP built specifically for freight
forwarders, customs brokers, shipping agents and 3PL operators. It replaces the
spreadsheets + WhatsApp + paper workflow that still dominates Cambodian logistics
firms with one platform that tracks a shipment from booking receipt all the way
to container return and job closure — plus a **self-hosted AI copilot** (chat,
document OCR, delay/ETA prediction, weekly reports, smart job assist) that works
**fully offline**.

Cambodia is a high-opportunity market because:

- **The trade is growing** — Cambodia's imports/exports keep rising (garments,
  footwear, travel goods, rice, cassava, bananas, rubber, and Chinese-driven
  manufacturing and infrastructure). More containers moving = more operational
  chaos for forwarders.
- **The tools are still manual** — the majority of logistics SMEs run jobs on
  Excel, paper and messaging apps. There is no dominant local ERP for this
  industry.
- **The market is underserved** — international ERPs (CargoWise, Magaya,
  Descartes) are expensive, English-centric and heavy; local generic ERPs don't
  understand the import/export workflow. CargoFlow is priced for local SMEs and
  speaks Khmer, English and Chinese.
- **AI is a differentiator** — a small, affordable, **offline** AI assistant is a
  compelling story for a market that values data privacy and cost efficiency.

**Strategy in one sentence:** Sell the *time and money saved* to the owner/ops
manager, not the software — via personal, high-trust sales (associations,
referrals, trade shows, banking/accounting partners) reinforced by a trilingual
digital funnel that turns "still on spreadsheets" forwarders into demos, trials
and paid plans.

## 2. Product Snapshot (what you are actually selling)

Sell outcomes, but know the features cold:

| Module | What it does for the customer |
|---|---|
| **Import management** | Booking receipt → approval → license/permit → trucking → vessel arrival → customs clearance → pickup → delivery → container return → closure. Full 18-step lifecycle, activity log. |
| **Export management** | Empty pickup → stuffing → port gate-in (EIR) → vessel departure → export clearance → closure. Mirrors the import process. |
| **Fleet management** | Trucks, trailers, drivers; document expiry tracking (IC, driving license); internal or outsourced (vendor) assignment. |
| **Invoicing & billing** | Per-job invoices, line items, COA mapping, tax, status workflow (DRAFT→ISSUED→PAID). |
| **Documents & templates** | Attach BLs, packing lists, permits per job; reusable shipment templates for routine cargo. |
| **Master data** | Customers, vendors, locations, items/services, company settings — with credit terms and COA codes. |
| **Search & alerts** | Global search across jobs/trucks/customers/vendors; real-time alerts for past ETA/ETD and expiring driver documents. |
| **Roles & approvals** | Admin/manager/staff roles, JWT security, role-gated approvals and destructive actions. |
| **Reports & dashboard** | Live KPI dashboard, calendar view, printable job cards, AI weekly operations report. |
| **AI copilot (offline)** | Natural-language chat over live data, OCR document extraction (BL/container/vessel fields), delay/ETA prediction, smart job next-step suggestions. Runs on a local Ollama model — no internet dependency, data never leaves the building. |

## 3. Market Analysis — Cambodia Landscape

### 3.1 Macro & trade context

- Cambodia's economy is driven by **garments/footwear/travel goods**, **agriculture**
  (rice, cassava, bananas, mangoes, rubber, cashews), and a fast-growing **Chinese-
  funded manufacturing & construction** sector.
- Key gateways: **Sihanoukville Autonomous Port (PAS)**, **Phnom Penh Autonomous
  Port (PPAP)**, inland ICDs, and the emerging **Kampot deep-water port** — plus
  growing cross-border trucking to Thailand and Vietnam.
- Trade agreements (**RCEP**, Cambodia–China FTA) keep boosting two-way container
  volumes.
- **Implication:** more consignments, more customs steps, more truck coordination
  → exactly the pain CargoFlow automates.

### 3.2 Buyer landscape (who actually decides)

- **Freight forwarders & customs brokers** (largest segment) — 5–50 staff, owner-
  operated, margins squeezed, operations on Excel/WhatsApp.
- **Shipping agents / NVOCC** — need vessel-level tracking, ETA/ETD alerts.
- **3PL & warehouse/logistics providers** — need fleet + delivery coordination.
- **Trading/import-export companies** (garment factories' logistics arms, rice
  exporters) — need customer/vendor master data, invoicing, approvals.

### 3.3 Digital maturity & buyer psychology

- Most SME owners are **price-sensitive and relationship-driven**. Deals are won
  on trust (introductions, associations, references), not ads.
- **Khmer language matters** for ops staff; **English** for proposals/international
  partners; **Chinese** for the growing Chinese business community.
- Common pain points to hit in every conversation:
  - "I don't know which truck/driver is available or whose license expires."
  - "We lose track of containers between customs and delivery."
  - "We re-type the same info into invoices and reports."
  - "It takes a week to see if a job is stuck."
- Common **objections**: 
  - cost (→ show ROI per job), 
  - change/learning (→ 1-week onboarding + Khmer support), 
  - data safety (→ offline/on-prem option), 
  - "we tried software before" (→ free trial + local demo with their own data style).

### 3.4 Competition

| Competitor type | Examples | Weakness vs. CargoFlow |
|---|---|---|
| Global freight ERPs | CargoWise (WiseTech), Magaya, Descartes | Expensive, complex, English-only, big implementation burden |
| Generic accounting ERPs | Local/SaaS accounting tools | No import/export workflow, no fleet, no customs lifecycle |
| Spreadsheets + apps | Excel + WhatsApp + email | No tracking, no alerts, no reporting, error-prone — this is your real "competitor" |

**Positioning:** *"The import/export ERP that speaks Khmer, runs offline with a
built-in AI copilot, and is priced for Cambodian forwarders."*

## 6. Pricing & Packaging Strategy for Cambodia

1. **Anchor in USD but bill in Riel-friendly terms** — keep USD pricing (the
   marketing site already uses it); offer quarterly/annual discounts
   (e.g., 2 months free on annual) to reduce churn risk.
2. **Free trial is your engine** — 14-day full Business-plan trial, no card
   required, with a guided demo in Khmer. Trial → paid conversion is the #1 KPI.
3. **Make Business the default ask** — it's where AI + invoicing live and where
   the "wow" demos happen. Use Starter as the low-friction entry.
4. **Local promo for year one** — e.g., first 20 Cambodian customers get 30% off
   the first year (creates urgency + early references).
5. **Enterprise = on-prem/private-cloud premium** — charge for the offline/on-prem
   capability and dedicated success manager.
6. **Avoid discounting habitually** — discount for references and annual plans
   only, or price perception collapses.

## 8. Sales Process (Lead → Close)

1. **Lead capture** — form/WhatsApp/Telegram/association intro.
2. **Discovery call (15–20 min, Khmer if needed)** — ask: how many jobs/month,
   what tools today, where is the pain (lost containers, missed ETAs, invoicing,
   license expiry)? Note their volume to propose the right plan.
3. **Live demo (30 min)** — use their language; walk an import job and an export
   job end-to-end; show the dashboard alert for a past ETA and the AI weekly
   report. Leave them with a screenshot PDF.
4. **Trial (14 days, Business plan)** — set it up for them in one call; import
   a few of their real containers so it "looks like their business" on day 1.
5. **Check-in (day 3 & 7)** — WhatsApp/Telegram: "how is it going? Need anything?"
   Answer fast (speed is your edge over international vendors).
6. **Close** — propose the right tier; offer annual discount; ask for a reference
   if they won't buy yet.
7. **Referral ask** — after 30 days of happy use, ask for 2 referrals.

**Sales assets needed:** Khmer + English + Chinese one-pagers, a 3-minute video
demo, a pricing sheet, a comparison vs. Excel/competitors, a trial setup checklist.

## 10. Onboarding & Retention (Churn kills growth)

- **Day-0:** assisted setup call (Khmer). Import their master data (customers,
  vendors, fleet) for them so the tool feels real immediately.
- **Week-1:** 2 training sessions for ops staff; provide Khmer quick-start guide.
- **Ongoing:** WhatsApp/Telegram support channel (respond < 1h), monthly "product
  tips" email in their language, quarterly check-in call.
- **Success metrics to watch:** weekly active jobs, trial→paid rate, NPS, churn.
- **Win-back:** before a customer cancels, offer training or a plan downgrade —
  most churn in Cambodia is "nobody used it" not "it's bad."

## 13. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Price sensitivity / "we'll keep Excel" | Free trial + ROI script + annual discount; prove time saved on their own data. |
| Change resistance of ops staff | Khmer training, do-it-for-them onboarding, quick support channel. |
| Internet/power instability | Offline AI + on-prem/private-cloud enterprise option; lightweight app. |
| Churn from non-usage | Assisted setup, weekly usage check-ins, win-back training. |
| Language gaps | Trilingual UI, Khmer/Chinese support & docs (already in product). |
| Competition from bigger global vendors | Speed, price, local language, offline AI, local support — don't fight them on feature lists. |
| Too broad, too fast | Focus P0 segment (PP + Sihanoukville forwarders) before expansion. |
