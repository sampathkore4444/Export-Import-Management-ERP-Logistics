FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is our data safe and private?

Yes. CargoFlow runs on your own infrastructure. All AI inference happens locally with self-hosted models — your shipment data never leaves your network.

Do I need an internet connection for the AI features?

No. The AI assistant, OCR extraction, predictions and reports run on local models via Ollama. When the AI service is offline, every feature falls back to a built-in rule-based mode.

Can I track both imports and exports?

Absolutely. Both flows are first-class citizens with their own status pipelines, documents, activity logs and reports — plus AI assistance on both.

Can we use our own trucks or outsource to vendors?

Both. Assign internal trucks, trailers and drivers directly, or mark a shipment as outsourced and link it to an approved vendor.

Is there a trial period?

Yes — every plan starts with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required, and a guided onboarding call is included.
